Important Notes

📍 Theory Classes

All theory classes are held in Room 401, Nazmol Hoque Bhaban. Students must be seated before the bell.

🔬 Lab / Sessional

All lab courses (even-numbered course codes) are conducted in their respective labs/workshops. Labs always run in the 7th or 8th period.

👥 Groups

Lab classes are split by group. Group A takes the 7th period slot; Group B takes the 8th period slot for most labs, alternating on some days.
